Browse the complete global board without getting stuck at a 100-message history boundary.

## Fixes

- **Continuous board history navigation.** Arrow keys, **j/k**, and the mouse wheel
  load the next or previous page when scrolling reaches a page boundary. Wrapped
  messages remain readable before navigation advances.
- **Fresh forward pagination.** Scrolling down and **PageDown** discover messages
  posted while live following is paused. Arrow and wheel navigation remain in
  history mode; **End** or **f** returns to live following.

Fixes [#19](https://github.com/Vuln-Inc/openraid/issues/19).
